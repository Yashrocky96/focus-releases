# Focus — Personal Time Intelligence for Windows

Wondering where your computer time went? Focus is an automatic time tracker for Windows that helps you understand your app and website usage without starting timers or reconstructing the day from memory.

Returning to interrupted work or preparing your own daily update? Revisit the apps and websites you used, then use Daily Notes to remember the context.

## Review your day. Notice patterns. Keep the context.

- **Today:** revisit the apps, websites and sessions that made up your day through daily summaries, categories and a chronological activity timeline.
- **Trends:** explore how your computer time is distributed across calendar weeks and months, rather than relying on memory.
- **Dated Notes:** capture an interruption, idea, decision or moment that an activity timeline cannot explain. Notes are optional context, not required time entry.

Focus is for understanding your own screen time—not employee monitoring, timesheets or app blocking.

## Local-first, offline activity tracking

While running, Focus records the active application, relevant window or page context, and session time. Detailed activity history is stored on your device during normal operation, and core tracking works without a cloud connection.

Optional external activity categorization runs only when you explicitly enable it.

## Get Focus for Windows

[Download the latest Windows release](https://github.com/Yashrocky96/focus-releases/releases/latest), open **Assets**, and run the `.exe` installer. Launch Focus to begin building your personal activity history.

Focus is early beta; bugs and categorization inaccuracies may occur.
